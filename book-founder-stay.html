Founder Housing

Book Founder Stay

Need a focused place to build in Detroit? We have short-stay room availability for founders who want to ship with support from the Hacker House community.

Who Can Stay

We can host either a solo founder or a small startup team of up to 8 founders.

Stay Length

Maximum stay is 3 nights, scheduled Sunday through Wednesday.

Check-In

Check-in starts at 4:00 PM. We will coordinate details after you submit interest.

Location

Detroit Hacker House is located near Wayne State University in Detroit's Art Center District.

Cost and Contribution

Flexible Contribution Model

There is no fixed nightly rate. We ask for one of the following:

  • A small donation
  • An introduction or relationship to someone who can help further our mission
  • Another contribution idea that aligns with founder support

We are open and happy to discuss what is possible.
